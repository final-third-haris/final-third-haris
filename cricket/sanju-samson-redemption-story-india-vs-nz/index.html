A technical and psychological assessment of Sanju Samson's evolution as an international top-order batter. We break down his balance at the crease, improved shot selection against hard lengths, temperament during middle-overs consolidation, and boundary acceleration against spin.

Key Tactical Takeaways & Discussion Points

  • Balance and head position enabling effortless lofted shots over extra cover
  • Adaptation against variations in pace and back-of-length bowling
  • Patience in strike rotation leading to calculated boundary expansion
  • Mental toughness and consistency under relentless selection scrutiny
