# Final Third

> Final Third is an independent sports journalism and tactical analysis platform created and hosted by Haris Nenmara, providing in-depth football match analysis, tactics and formations, Kerala Blasters coverage, and cricket match analysis in Malayalam.

## Website & Core Sections

- [Final Third](https://www.finalthird-haris.com/): The official website featuring latest tactical breakdowns and video releases.
- [Football & Cricket Video Catalog](https://www.finalthird-haris.com/#videos): Searchable archive of football match analysis, tactical debriefs, and cricket stories.
- [Final Third Shorts](https://www.finalthird-haris.com/#shorts): Short-form tactical highlights and sports analysis reels.
- [About Haris Nenmara](https://www.finalthird-haris.com/#about): Background, journalistic perspective, and editorial philosophy of host Haris Nenmara.
- [Partnership & Brand Inquiries](https://www.finalthird-haris.com/#sponsor): Information on media sponsorships, video shoutouts, and brand collaborations.
- [Contact](https://www.finalthird-haris.com/#contact): Direct contact channels including WhatsApp and email.

## Official Channels

- [Final Third YouTube Channel](https://www.youtube.com/@FinalThird_HarisNenmara): Official video analysis channel.
- [Final Third Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/finalthird.official/): Matchday clips and analysis updates.

## Topics Covered

- **Football Analysis:** Match reviews, tactical approaches, formations, European leagues (Champions League, Premier League, La Liga), Indian football, and Kerala Blasters (ISL).
- **Cricket Analysis:** Match breakdowns, tactical insights, player performances, Sanju Samson analysis, and international tournament coverage.
- **Language & Region:** High-quality sports journalism primarily presented in Malayalam for sports fans in Kerala and the global Malayali diaspora.
