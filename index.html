JOURNALIST & ANALYST
THE JOURNALIST BEHIND THE GAME

HARIS NENMARA (ഹാരിസ് നെന്മാറ)

“Looking beyond the scoreline. Uncovering the story behind the game.”

Haris Nenmara is a journalist, sports reporter, and analyst passionate about exploring the stories that shape the world of sport. As the face behind Final Third, he brings journalistic perspective and thoughtful analysis to the games, personalities, and moments that captivate millions of fans.

With professional experience in journalism and sports reporting, Haris looks beyond the headlines to examine the bigger picture. His coverage spans football and cricket, exploring tactical approaches, team performances, individual brilliance, and the decisive moments that shape a match.

From the intricate tactical battles of football to the strategic contests of cricket, his approach is driven by curiosity, context, and a desire to understand not just what happened, but why it happened.

Through Final Third, Haris brings fans closer to the game with insightful analysis, engaging discussions, and a deeper look at the stories behind the results.

Because sport is more than numbers on a scoreboard. It's about the strategy, the pressure, the people, and the moments that make every contest worth watching.
